Short Film  ·  In Development

RUE'S
RITUAL

A short film that traces the quiet collapse of a teenage girl, contrasting who she was against who she's become...

Coming-of-age Teen Drama Dark Themes Survival
001
Characters

Rue

Rue used to be the kind of person people gravitated toward... Sharp, warm, someone you wanted in your corner. Now she moves through the halls like she's trying to disappear, and most days she succeeds.
She works nights, walks alone, and keeps the world at arm's length with headphones and silence. She's not looking for a saviour, not asking for help, and not interested in your pity.

But underneath the walls she's built is someone who still notices things, still feels things, even when feeling them costs her everything

Ashley

Ashley is the guy you'd expect to have everything figured out, good looking, sporty, everyone's mate.
And mostly, he is. But there's one person he can't quite be casual about, no matter how hard he plays it cool.

Themes

Identity & Body

The HRT clearance as a quiet act of self-reclamation buried inside everything else falling apart.

Ritual & Numbness

The night walk as both self-destruction and the only structure Rue has left.

Visibility & Shame

From someone seen to someone who hides. The exposé as the moment the world made that permanent.

Empathy as Disruption

The ending argues that Rue's capacity to feel for someone else is what interrupts her own dissolution.
